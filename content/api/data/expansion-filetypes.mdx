Some specific platforms and [primary filetypes](/api/data/filetypes) may support additional expansion files.

| Platform                          | Primary Filetype | Expansion Filetype | Expansion Filetype Description |
|-----------------------------------|------------------|--------------------|--------------------------------|
| Android, Quest, Pico, VIVE Mobile | apk              | obb                | Android OBB Expansion File     |
| Xbox Series X/S                   | xvc              | ekb                | Encrypted Key Blob             |
