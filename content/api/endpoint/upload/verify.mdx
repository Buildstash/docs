The upload verification endpoint is the final step in the build upload process, for both single part and multipart uploads. It verifies that all parts have been successfully uploaded and finalizes the build record in Buildstash. Requires `builds:create` scope.

<APIPage document={"./openapi.json"} operations={[{"path":"/upload/verify","method":"post"}]} webhooks={[]} hasHead={false} />

### Notes

- For multipart uploads, all parts with ETags must be included in the `multipart_chunks` array
- ETags must match exactly what was returned from the upload requests
- Some builds may take a short delay to process, indicated by `pending_processing: true`. Follow processing with [/upload/status](/api/endpoint/upload/get-upload-status)
- Verifying is safe to retry - if the upload was already verified, the same successful response is returned, without redoing anything
- Uploads that were cancelled, failed, or weren't verified within 48 hours of being requested can't be verified
