## Introduction

The audit log (shown in the app as **Activity log**) provides a record of changes made within your workspace. This helps workspace owners and admins track who did what, and when — whether changes were made through the web interface or via the API.

To view the audit log, go to **Workspace settings** > **Activity log**.

## What is logged

The audit log captures actions performed by workspace members, including changes to:

- Applications, builds, and releases
- Portals and distribution groups
- Workspace settings, labels, streams, and custom targets
- API keys, connections, and webhooks
- Workspace members and roles

Each entry shows a description of the action, the member who performed it, when it occurred, and the affected resource.

## Filtering

You can filter the audit log by:

- **Member** — show activity for a specific workspace member
- **Application** — limit to actions within a particular application
- **Target** — filter by resource type (such as Build, Release, or Portal)
- **Action** — filter by event type (such as created, updated, or deleted)

Use **Reset filters** to clear all active filters.

## Retention

How far back you can view activity depends on your [subscription plan](https://buildstash.com/pricing). Each plan includes a defined log history window — for example, 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Ultra.

Entries outside your plan's retention window are not accessible. Upgrading your plan extends the available history.

## Permissions

The audit log is available to workspace **Owners** and **Admins** only.
